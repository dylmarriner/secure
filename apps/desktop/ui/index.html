Create your local vault

Choose a passphrase. It never leaves this device and is never sent anywhere. It wraps a randomly generated vault key that actually protects your data - losing this passphrase means losing access to this vault, by design.

Vault locked

Enter your passphrase to unlock this device's encrypted vault.

Ghost Session

A disposable session with no connection to your vault. Nothing it does is written to this device's normal storage - no history, no contacts, no drafts - and it leaves nothing behind for a restart to recover.

What this cannot promise: swap, hibernation, a crash dump, EDR, or screenshots remain outside this application's control while the session is running.

Ghost Session active

You are in a disposable session. Your normal vault stays locked and untouched - nothing here can read or write it. Ending this session destroys its key immediately; there is nothing to recover afterwards, by design.

Vault unlocked

This build exposes lifecycle status, lock and a managed Tor transport through the Tauri IPC boundary. Messaging and attachments are deliberately absent until their audited implementations land - there is nothing to send over Tor yet.

Tor transport

Tor is never started automatically. Bootstrapping can take anywhere from a few seconds to over a minute depending on your network.

Destroy this identity

This permanently deletes the key that protects your vault. Every message, contact and attachment on this device becomes undecryptable - by you as well. There is no undo, no recovery phrase and no support route back. Your passphrase will not help afterwards.

What this cannot promise: copies already delivered to the people you messaged, data your operating system wrote to swap, hibernation or a crash dump, anything captured by a backup or filesystem snapshot, or bytes retained by your drive's own firmware.

A replacement is a genuinely new vault: a fresh random key that has no relationship to the destroyed one, so its passphrase is unrelated and the old passphrase is simply wrong against it. It starts empty, with no contacts and no history. It is not a disguise - anyone who knows this application knows a new vault can be created at any time, so treat it as starting over, not as a cover story.

Identity destroyed

The vault key has been destroyed and the encrypted database removed. Nothing on this device can decrypt the former contents. Restart Secure to finish setting up a new identity.

No analytics. No remote assets. No direct frontend networking.
